### Decisions

- **The guard ships as a validator, not as spec-local tooling.** Rejected: leaving `CONTRIBUTING.md`'s amended sentence to do the work alone, which is what #61 did — the convention regressed silently inside the branch that wrote it down. Also rejected: folding it into `check-templates.py`, whose docstring commits it to one file and one question.
- **Scope is every tracked `.md`, not shipped paths.** Restating the rule by *path* is the same category of mistake as stating it by *delimiter*, which is the bug. The two answers scan the same set today.
- **The rule does not travel into the skills.** Templates come out unwrapped and consumers do as they like. `.steering/product.md` draws the line at "not a document generator"; a prose-style instruction in shipped skills is the harness taking a position on how consumers write.
- **AC6 was amended mid-run, alone and ahead of T4, per #59.** It asked for rendering equivalence. A fenced block's content is literal, so the joins genuinely change what a fence renders — the criterion would have had to be waived on the two files it existed to judge. It now asks for word-stream identity inside the changed fences and byte identity outside them.
- **The independence claim was narrowed twice and is worth stating once properly.** `check-markdown-fences.py` first said it shared *no code* with `unwrap.py`. `OPENER`'s first five alternatives are `NEW_BLOCK`'s five reordered. The second version said the other three had no counterpart in `NEW_BLOCK` — true, and misleading, since the thematic break and `{{SLOT}}` have counterparts elsewhere in `unwrap.py` (`:70`, `:30`). Only the bare `<` is genuinely unique. What is actually not shared is the decision procedure.
- **The counters behind the coverage invariant now share the scan's tag extraction.** They disagreed about ` ```markdown title="x" `, tripping the invariant with "0 present but 1 scanned" — a false block with a self-contradictory diagnosis, which is how a guard gets switched off. Sharing *tag semantics* is required; the invariant's independent variable is *coverage*, which it still computes with no nesting awareness.
