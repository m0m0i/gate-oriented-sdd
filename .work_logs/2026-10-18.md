## 2026-10-18 — m0m0i, #316 implemented: the gates find a branch's spec from what it carries

### Summary

`316-gates-find-a-spec-only-by-branch-name` (#316) went from an approved spec to a CLEAN review and the 0.35.0 bump in one session. It took four tasks, three spec amendments committed alone ahead of their code, and two review rounds, and ended with 378 test-gates cases green. Every reader used to find a branch's spec only at `.specs/<branch>/spec.md`. A branch named any other way (`feature/12-x`, a typo, or a spec archived on its own branch) passed the gate, the scan and CI with no review. The readers now take a branch's specs from `gate_branch_specs`: the name match, plus every spec the branch adds against its merge base with the base.

### Decisions

- **A union, never a replacement.** The name match stays beside the adds, so no reader checks fewer specs than it did.
- **The shipped skip asks every listed slug, not any.** That keeps a squash-merged `feature/12-x` out of the scan without reopening case 134's carried-on fail-open.
- **A failed listing still judges the name match, and blocks besides**, in the gate, the scan and CI alike.
- **Accepted, and recorded in the spec:**
  - a branch stacked on another unmerged spec branch blocks on the lower spec's stale receipt until it is rebased;
  - an unreviewed branch not named for its spec, merged into a local `main` and not pushed, is silent locally, as before #316.
- **The scan's extra merge-base walk was left in place.** Folding it into the ancestry check would save about half the added cost. That optimization belongs with the batched history walk already recorded in BACKLOG row 1.
