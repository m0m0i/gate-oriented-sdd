### Decisions

- **The clock, not the HEAD commit time.** #105 offered both and reported the second as free —
  `reviewed_at` from `git log`, "already on every allow-list". It is not: the listed form is
  `git log --oneline <base>...HEAD`, which prints no date, so that fix needed a new command on
  all five lists too. At equal cost it also makes the field derivable from `reviewed_sha` and
  stops it recording when the review ran. Rules out treating the two proposals as a cost trade.
- **The guard went into `check-receipt-schema.py`, not a tenth validator.** It already owns the
  contract-to-receipt relationship and already byte-compares the mirror; #54 records that the
  guard count argues against growing. Rules out `scripts/check-reviewer-allowlists.py`.
- **The pairing releases as well as binds.** A field the contract requires must have a command
  on every list, *and* a command demanded of every list must still be required by the contract —
  case 44. A guard that outlives its reason is one that gets switched off.
- **`reviewed_sha` became enforced rather than asserted** (reviewer MEDIUM). It had been a
  comment saying the command was already on every list, which is the root cause of this very
  issue written as a comment. Enforcing it closed the other half of the recurrence claim: case 48
  catches a field arriving with no producer, case 50 catches a producer being deleted from a list.
- **`PRODUCERS`' `None` has two kinds, not three** (reviewer MEDIUM, the best finding of the five
  rounds). The middle category — "comes from a command already on every list" — had no members
  and must never have any, and it was the guidance a maintainer would read at the exact moment of
  classifying a new field. Left as written, it would have reproduced #105 past the guard built to
  prevent it.
- **The path-count claim was fixed in five places and left false in a sixth.** `CONTRIBUTING.md`
  was found by the reviewer, not by the first survey, which searched a hand-listed set of
  directories. The re-enumeration is `git grep` across all tracked files. Listing one more
  directory would only have moved where the next gap is.
- **Records stay records**, per the #102 precedent: `docs/verified.md`'s four `54/0` rows, the
  `.work_logs/` mentions, and the `observations.md` validator line in all eleven spec directories
  that carry one. Each says what a run observed on its date.
- **Accepted rather than fixed, with reasons.** The reviewer's note that "known to the reviewer
  from its own run" reads narrowly against a field obtained by *reading* rather than generating:
  such a field is correctly `None` either way, the completeness check still forces an entry and a
  comment, and the reviewer advised against touching the sentence for that alone. Also that case
  49 pins the floor's headline but not its actionable second line: pinning every sentence of
  every message buys brittleness at a poor rate.
