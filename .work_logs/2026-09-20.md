- **`sprint` writes back; the other two doors are compared, not written.** Its rule narrowed from "writes no document" to "creates no parallel record" — which forbids a second source of truth without forbidding the correction of the first — and step 6 records the issue numbers on the rows it took. A merge closing an issue and an issue filed outside a grooming belong to no skill, so nothing can be instructed to write them. Rejected adding `implement` as a second writer.
- **#182 was placed at row 2 by the user's ordering judgment**, pushing rows 2–23 to 3–24. Placing an issue the check reported is not the re-groom the spec ruled out; no order was revisited and no reasoning rewritten.
- **Grooming records are not renumbered; live references are.** `## What changed at this grooming` still says "#177 takes row 2" and "#74 is covered by row 8's cell" — true when written, now rows 3 and 9. A record corrected by a later change stops being a record. Round 3 caught the first statement of this boundary over-claiming: it named a whole subsection as live when the renumber had, correctly, been selective inside it. The test is the reference, not the section.
- **T2's cases were not red, and the record says so.** T1's commit already carried every fail-closed path.
