### Decisions
- **The work log stays in the feature PR; archive runs only when asked.** The alternative — a per-session housekeeping PR carrying the work log and a swept archive — was rejected because it removes a bookkeeping pull request by adding one. Rules out any future claim that the work log belongs on its own branch; it rides the branch it describes.

- **`CONTRACTS` grew 7 → 9 against its own "keep the list short".** Argued in the spec: both pinned sentences are ones whose silent deletion restores the second pull request, and `(review PR)` surviving #30 by four months is the evidence that unguarded prose rules rot here. The docstring now carries the number and the condition a tenth entry must meet — the reviewer's point being that the only brake previously written down lived in a spec headed for `_archive/`.
- **The `implement` needle keeps its conjunction rather than splitting into two entries.** One conjoined string already asserts both halves, so splitting buys zero coverage while spending the budget the docstring's new limit was just given. Recorded in the spec because the init precedent points the other way and would otherwise re-open it.

Deferred to the next commit that touches that file, per the reviewer's own advice.
