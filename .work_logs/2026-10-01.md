### Decisions

- **Additions go in the reviewer file, not in a fifth general purpose.** A fifth purpose would still deny projects their own sources, and would give every reviewer a source only some need. Dropping the list for "any read-only command" was rejected too: it loses the point of the list, which is that a reviewer's reach can be read off its file.
- **Read-only means "writes nothing the validators do not already write".** It does not mean "nothing in the tree". The contract already says a reviewer never creates a file, and a narrower wording would have let a temporary directory through, which would quietly decide #185.
- **"Named" asks for a command form, not a tool** (`git show`, not `git`). That is what makes "scoped" auditable from the allow-list.
- **No guard pins the clause.** All four conditions are judgments, which belong in the reviewer layer. `check-skill-contracts.py` is at its cap of twenty-five and its subject is skills.
- **#222 is cited in row 17's `Why here`, not its `Item` cell.** #219's `Item` citation is what #221 is cleaning up now, and the tracker check is green on both sides of the merge for a `Why here` citation.
- **Patch.** #105 changed the same section and shipped as 0.4.4.
