### Decisions

- **Redefine a task rather than teach the gate a new word.** Four options were on the issue and
  none was obviously right. #10's precedent decided it: that bug did not teach the gate to
  recognise a half-task, it redefined a task so half-tasks cannot exist, then pinned the
  definition with a guard. Option 2's post-review marker was rejected for adding vocabulary whose
  omission re-opens the hole exactly as today — it moves the failure rather than removing it.
  Option 1 is the dishonest tick. Option 4 cannot work: mid-implementation there is no receipt at
  all, so an arming rule based on receipt staleness would block every ordinary turn.
- **Both guards, not one.** They fail in opposite directions — the instruction being edited away,
  and a spec written past an instruction still present — and only the skill-contracts entry
  reaches consumers, since `scripts/` is not shipped and `skills/` is. The argument that guard's
  docstring demands of a tenth entry: a deleted step is visible in a diff, but its significance is
  not, and what its removal restores is a gate that says nothing rather than a defect anyone sees.
- **Positional matching, with a named edge.** The directive is matched anywhere and later clauses
  only at their start, because `— after the review` schedules while `— a live spec whose … after
  the review` describes. This spec's own T1 and T3 are the adversarial fixtures. The two tempting
  alternatives were both rejected: narrowing the phrase list until this file passes tunes the
  guard to one document, and exempting the spec that owns the guard exempts the file most likely
  to get it wrong. The over-reach edge — a compliant task whose directive names the review — is
  documented in the guard with the rephrasing that clears it.
- **0.5.0, minor rather than patch.** #47's precedent would make prose under `skills/` a patch,
  but this moves where the bump sits in the flow and #52 took a minor for a flow change. On the
  author's word, a major is reserved for the minimum/full install switch.
- **Accepted rather than fixed, with the reason.** The third round's only finding is a
  129-character comment line among 92-character neighbours in `check-templates.py` — purely
  presentational, and fixing it would re-stale a CLEAN receipt and buy a fourth review round for a
  reflow. It should ride along with the next change to that file.
- **`.specs/_archive/` is excluded by name, not by depth.** #105's T5 is exactly the forbidden
  shape and is a record of what was done; rewriting a record to satisfy a later guard destroys its
  value as evidence (#102). Flagged twice as un-mutation-testable before a fixture at
  `.specs/_archive/spec.md` made deleting the exemption turn the case red.
